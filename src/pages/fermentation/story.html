Fermentation.

Every bottle in the cabinet starts the same way. With yeast, sugar and a little bit of time.

It's called fermentation. And it's way older than we are.

The Short Version
Yeast + Sugar = CO₂ + Alcohol
What happens
Yeast, a tiny microorganism, eats sugar. It gives off alcohol, plus carbon dioxide (the bubbles).
The sugar
Comes from plants: fruit, grain, honey, sugarcane, agave. Grapes make wine. Grain makes beer.
And spirits?
Every spirit starts as something fermented. Distill it, and you get whiskey, brandy, rum or tequila.
How old?
It happens in nature all on its own. People have been making fermented drinks on purpose for at least 9,000 years.
