Garibaldi Recipe

The Cocktail Menu · Low & No-Proof

Garibaldi

Makes 1 drink

  • 1 ½ oz Campari
  • 4 oz orange juice (fresh squeezed, and fluffed)

Glass: tall glass, over ice · Garnish: an orange wedge

Two ingredients.

One very good trick.

It's named for Giuseppe Garibaldi, the general who helped unite Italy in the 1800s: red Campari for his red-shirted volunteers in the north, orange juice for the oranges of Sicily in the south.

For a long time, it was just Campari and orange juice. Then Dante, a bar in New York, started making it with “fluffy” orange juice, squeezed fresh and whipped full of air, and turned it into a modern classic.

You can fluff it at home: blend fresh orange juice for a few seconds, or shake it hard without ice. It comes out light and silky, with a foamy orange head.
