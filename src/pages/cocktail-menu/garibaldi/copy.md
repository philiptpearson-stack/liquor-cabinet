Garibaldi Recipe
The Cocktail Menu · Low & No-Proof
Garibaldi
Makes − 1 + drink
1 ½ oz Campari
4 oz orange juice (fluffed)
**Glass:** tall glass, over ice · **Garnish:** an orange wedge
Two ingredients.
One *very* good trick.
It's named for Giuseppe Garibaldi, the general who helped unite Italy in the 1800s: red Campari for his red-shirted volunteers in the north, orange juice for the oranges of Sicily in the south.
For a long time, it was just Campari and orange juice. Then Dante, a bar in New York, started making it with “fluffy” orange juice, squeezed fresh and whipped full of air, and turned it into a modern classic.
You can fluff it at home: blend orange juice for a few seconds, or shake it hard without ice. It comes out light and silky, with a foamy orange head.
Here's how you make one:

**Ice.** Fill a tall glass with ice.
**Campari.**
**Top with fluffy orange juice.** Blend the juice for a few seconds (or shake it hard, without ice) until it's light and foamy. Then pour it in.
**Garnish.** An orange wedge.
