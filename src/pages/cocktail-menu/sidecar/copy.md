The Sidecar showed up around the end of World War I. Paris and London both claim it.
Cognac, orange liqueur and lemon: rich, bright and dangerously easy to drink.
*It's the Golden Ratio, with brandy: two parts cognac, one part orange liqueur, one part lemon.*

Here's how you make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Classics
Sidecar
Makes − 1 + drink
2 oz cognac (or another brandy)
1 oz orange liqueur
1 oz lemon juice (fresh squeezed)
**Glass:** coupe · **Garnish:** orange twist
**Combine ingredients.** Add the cognac, orange liqueur and lemon juice to your shaker.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake hard, until frosty cold.
**Serve up.** Strain into a chilled coupe, no ice.
**Garnish.** An orange twist.
Feeling fancy? Rim the glass with sugar before you pour.
