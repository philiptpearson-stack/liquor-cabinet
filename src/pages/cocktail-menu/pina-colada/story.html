Piña Colada Recipe

The Cocktail Menu · Tiki & Tropical

Piña Colada

Makes 1 drink

  • 2 oz white rum
  • 1 ½ oz cream of coconut (like Coco López)
  • 2 oz pineapple juice
  • ½ oz lime juice

Glass: highball, over crushed ice
Garnish: a pineapple wedge and a cherry

Important Note: you want cream of coconut — like Coco López. Not coconut milk, and not coconut cream. They're different!

As Puerto Rican as Bad Bunny.

Officially.

The Piña Colada was born in the 1950s at a Hilton hotel in San Juan, and in 1978 it became the island's official drink.
