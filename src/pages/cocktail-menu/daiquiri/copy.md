The Cocktail Menu: ***Daiquiri.***
Forget the blender.
The original Daiquiri was named for a beach near Santiago, Cuba, and it was just three ingredients: rum, lime and sugar.
Simple perfection.
Cold, bright and tart.
*It's The Golden Ratio in its simplest form: two parts rum, one part simple syrup, one part lime juice.*
→ The Golden Ratio
This is how you make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Classics
Daiquiri
Makes − 1 + drink
2 oz white rum
1 oz simple syrup
1 oz lime juice (fresh squeezed)
**Glass:** coupe · **Garnish:** lime wheel
**Combine ingredients.** Add the rum, simple syrup and lime juice to your shaker.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake hard, until the shaker is frosty cold.
**Serve up.** Strain into a chilled coupe, no ice.
**Garnish.** A lime wheel on the rim.
