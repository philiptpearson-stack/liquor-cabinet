The Cocktail Menu: ***Daiquiri.***
Forget the blender.
The real Daiquiri is named for a beach near Santiago, Cuba, and it's just three ingredients: rum, lime and sugar.
Cold, bright and tart. Bartenders love it, because there's nowhere for a sloppy drink to hide.
*It's The Golden Ratio at its simplest: two parts rum, one part simple syrup, one part lime.*
→ The Golden Ratio
Here's how you make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Classics
Daiquiri
Makes − 1 + drink
2 oz white rum
1 oz simple syrup
1 oz lime juice (fresh squeezed)
**Glass:** coupe · **Garnish:** lime wheel
**Combine ingredients.** Add the rum, simple syrup and lime juice to your shaker.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake hard, until the shaker is frosty cold.
**Serve up.** Strain into a chilled coupe, no ice.
**Garnish.** A lime wheel on the rim.
