The name is a joke.
The drink is not.
Bartender Sam Ross made it in 2005 at Milk & Honey in New York, the same bar where the Gold Rush was born a few years earlier.
It's the Gold Rush's Scotch-drinking sibling: blended Scotch, lemon, and honey with a kick of fresh ginger.
Then comes the trick: a float of smoky Islay Scotch on top, so the smoke reaches your nose before the drink reaches your lips.
Here's how you make one:

Penicillin
Makes − 1 + drink
2 oz blended Scotch
¾ oz lemon juice
¾ oz honey-ginger syrup
¼ oz Islay Scotch, to float
**Glass:** rocks, over one big ice cube · **Garnish:** candied ginger
Honey-ginger syrup: simmer equal parts honey and water with a few slices of fresh ginger, then strain. (Or use ½ oz honey syrup and ¼ oz fresh ginger juice.)
**Combine ingredients.** Add the blended Scotch, lemon juice and honey-ginger syrup to your shaker.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake hard, until frosty cold.
**Strain over a big cube.** Strain into a rocks glass over one large ice cube.
**Float the Islay.** Pour it gently over the back of a spoon, so it sits on top.
**Garnish.** A piece of candied ginger.
