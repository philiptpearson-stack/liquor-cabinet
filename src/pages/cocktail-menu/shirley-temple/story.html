The Cocktail Menu · Low & No-Proof

Shirley Temple

Makes 1 drink

  • 6 oz ginger ale
  • ½ oz lime juice (fresh squeezed)
  • ½ oz grenadine

Glass: tall glass, over ice · Garnish: a cherry (or three)

A drink for the kids' table.

No alcohol at all.

It's named for Shirley Temple, the biggest child star of the 1930s. Restaurants from Hollywood to Honolulu claim they made the first one, so the young star would have something fancy to drink at the grown-ups' table.

Shirley Temple herself was not a fan. Years later, she called them “saccharine, icky drinks.”

She had a point. So we add a squeeze of lime: a little sour, to balance all that sweet. Balance!
