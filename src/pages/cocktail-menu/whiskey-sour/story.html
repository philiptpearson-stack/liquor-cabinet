The Cocktail Menu: Whiskey Sour.

Where it all starts.

The Whiskey Sour was already in print in 1862, in the very first American bartender's guide.

Bourbon, lemon and sugar, with an optional egg white for a silky foam on top.

It's The Golden Ratio, the classic way: two parts bourbon, one part simple syrup, one part lemon.

→ The Golden Ratio

Here's how to make one:

The Cocktail Menu · Classics

Whiskey Sour

Makes 1 drink

  • 2 oz bourbon
  • 1 oz simple syrup
  • 1 oz lemon juice (fresh squeezed)
  • 1 egg white (optional)

Glass: coupe · Garnish: cherry

Pasteurized egg whites (from a carton) work just as well as fresh. For a vegan foam, swap in ¾ oz aquafaba, the liquid from a can of chickpeas.
