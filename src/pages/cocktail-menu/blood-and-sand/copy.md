Okay, here's the thing.
People either like it, or they really don't.
And even the ones that like it usually want to fix it.
It's a classic formula: like the Last Word, or the Paper Plane, it's a 1:1:1:1.
That makes it super easy to build.
It also makes it sort of...*bland*.
So if you're one of the ones who likes it but wants it fixed (like we are), do what we do.
Add more whisky.
Here's how you make it:
The Cocktail Menu · Arcane Oddities
Blood and Sand
Makes − 1 + drink
¾ oz Scotch (or more!)
¾ oz sweet vermouth
¾ oz cherry liqueur (like Cherry Heering)
¾ oz orange juice
**Glass:** coupe
**Garnish:** an orange twist
**Combine ingredients.** Equal parts Scotch, sweet vermouth, cherry liqueur and orange juice, into your shaker.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake hard, until frosty cold.
**Strain.** Strain into a chilled coupe.
**Garnish.** An orange twist.
