Blood and Sand

Makes 1 drink

  • ¾ oz Scotch (or more!)
  • ¾ oz sweet vermouth
  • ¾ oz cherry liqueur (like Cherry Heering)
  • ¾ oz orange juice

Glass: coupe
Garnish: an orange twist
