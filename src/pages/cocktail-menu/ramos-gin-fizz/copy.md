Ramos Gin Fizz Recipe

Ramos Gin Fizz
Makes − 1 + drink
2 oz gin
½ oz lemon juice (fresh squeezed)
½ oz lime juice (fresh squeezed)
¾ oz simple syrup
1 oz heavy cream
1 egg white
3 dashes orange flower water
1 oz soda water, to top
**Glass:** highball, no ice · **Garnish:** none (the foam is the show)
Pasteurized egg whites (from a carton) work just as well as fresh. Chill the soda water: the colder it is, the higher the foam.
Shake it.
Then keep shaking.
Henry C. Ramos created his gin fizz at his New Orleans saloon in the late 1880s, and it took a *lot* of shaking.
During Mardi Gras in 1915, his bar had a line of 35 “shaker boys,” passing the drinks down the line and shaking them as they went.
All that shaking whips the cream and egg white into a light, fluffy foam. Then a splash of cold soda lifts the foam right up over the top of the glass.
At home, a good minute or two of hard shaking will do. (Your arms will feel it. It's worth it.)

Pasteurized egg whites (from a carton) work just as well as fresh. Chill the soda water: the colder it is, the higher the foam.
**Combine ingredients.** Add everything except the soda water to your shaker.
**Dry shake.** Shake hard, without ice, for about 15 seconds. That's what starts the foam.
**Shake. And keep shaking.** Add ice and shake as hard as you can, for a good minute or two.
**Strain.** Strain into a chilled highball glass, no ice.
**Top with soda water.** Pour it in slowly, and watch the foam rise.
