The Cocktail Menu · Low & No-Proof
Ruby Slipper
Makes − 1 + drink
1 oz lime juice
1 oz
simple syrup
1 oz cranberry juice
3 oz 7UP
**Glass:** cocktail glass
**Garnish:** a sugared rim
**Sugar the rim.** Rub a lime wedge around the outside of the rim of a chilled cocktail glass, then roll it in sugar.
**Combine ingredients.** Add the lime juice, simple syrup and cranberry juice to your shaker.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake hard, until frosty cold.
**Add the 7UP.** Right into the shaker. Give it a gentle swirl.
**Strain.** Strain into the sugared glass.
