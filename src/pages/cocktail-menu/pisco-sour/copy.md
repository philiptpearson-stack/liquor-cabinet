It's a Whiskey Sour, with Peruvian brandy!
Invented by a prominent Mormon from Utah.
**For real!**
In 1902 Victor Morris left Salt Lake City for Peru — where the story goes he made his first Pisco Sour at a party, *after the whiskey had run out.*
Eventually, that Pisco Sour became the signature drink at his bar, Morris' Bar, in Lima.
Then it became Peru's national drink.
Here's how you make one:

Pisco Sour
Makes − 1 + drink

3 drops Angostura bitters, on top
**Glass:** coupe
**Garnish:** a few drops of Angostura bitters on the foam
Pasteurized egg whites (from a carton) work just as well as fresh. For a vegan foam, swap in ¾ oz aquafaba, the liquid from a can of chickpeas.
**Combine ingredients.** Add the pisco, lime juice, simple syrup and egg white to your shaker.
**Dry shake.** Shake hard, without ice, for about 15 seconds. That's what whips the egg white into foam.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake hard, until frosty cold.
**Strain.** Strain into a chilled coupe, and let the foam settle on top.
**Garnish.** A few drops of Angostura on the foam. (Drag a toothpick through them, if you're feeling fancy.)
