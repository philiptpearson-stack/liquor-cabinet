Pisco Sour

Makes 1 drink

  • 2 oz pisco
  • 1 oz lime juice (fresh squeezed)
  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • 1 egg white
  • 3 drops Angostura bitters, on top

Glass: coupe · Garnish: a few drops of Angostura bitters on the foam

Pasteurized egg whites (from a carton) work just as well as fresh. For a vegan foam, swap in ¾ oz aquafaba, the liquid from a can of chickpeas.

Peru's national drink.

Invented by a guy from Utah.

Victor Morris left Salt Lake City for Peru in 1902, to work on a railroad. He stayed, married, and opened Morris' Bar in Lima.

He said he first made his sour with pisco, the local grape brandy, when the whiskey ran out at a party.

One of his bartenders, Mario Bruiget, is believed to have added the egg white and bitters. That's the version Peru drinks today.
