Hurricane

Makes 1 drink

  • 1 oz white rum
  • 1 oz dark rum
  • 1 oz lemon juice
  • 1 oz passion fruit syrup

Glass: hurricane glass, over ice
Garnish: an orange slice and a cherry

Can't find passion fruit syrup? Make it: stir equal parts passion fruit purée and sugar over low heat until the sugar dissolves. Let it cool.

Got too much rum?

Make a Hurricane.

Pat O'Brien's started out as a speakeasy in New Orleans' French Quarter. The password? “Storm's brewin'.”

In the 1940s, the story goes, liquor distributors made O'Brien buy cases and cases of rum before they'd sell him a few cases of whiskey.

So he had a lot of rum. He mixed it with passion fruit and lemon, poured it into tall glasses shaped like hurricane lamps, and gave it away to sailors.

It's been a French Quarter staple ever since.
