Hurricane Recipe

The Cocktail Menu · Tiki & Tropical

Hurricane

Makes 1 drink

  • 4 oz dark rum
  • 2 oz lemon juice
  • 2 oz passion fruit syrup

Glass: hurricane glass, over ice
Garnish: an orange slice and a cherry

Can't find passion fruit syrup? Make your own: the recipe's on our syrups page.

It was a big ass drink meant to sell tourists a LOT of rum.

That's why it goes in that crazy glass.

The glass is shaped like a hurricane lantern.

Imagine it's 1972. You've just left Pat O'Brien's (the bar where the Hurricane was invented), and you're sipping one as you stroll around the French Quarter.

The drink hits you like a hurricane.

But don't worry. It's New Orleans.
