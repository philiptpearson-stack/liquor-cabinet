Hot Buttered Rum Recipe

Hot Buttered Rum
Makes − 1 + drink
2 oz dark rum
1 oz buttered rum batter (about 2 tablespoons; see below)
4 oz hot water
**Glass:** warm mug
**Garnish:** a cinnamon stick
Batter (for about 12 drinks): beat ½ cup softened butter with ½ cup brown sugar, 1 tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp nutmeg, ¼ tsp ground cloves and a pinch of salt. It keeps in the fridge for a couple of weeks.
Yes, there's butter in it.
Yes, that might seem sort of yucky.
But it's actually the point!
In colonial America, rum was everywhere. On cold nights, people drank it hot, with sugar and spice, and sometimes a pat of butter.
We actually don't know why they would have wanted to do that.
With the butter.
Was it like that fad a few years back where people were putting butter in their coffee??
Believe it or not, though, in this drink? It's actually awesome. Just make yourself a batter: butter, brown sugar and warm spices, beaten together and kept in the fridge. Drop a spoonful in a mug with rum and hot water, and you've got the Holiday Season in a glass!

**Batter and rum.** Add a spoonful of batter and the rum to a warm mug.
**Top with hot water.** Just off the boil.
**Stir.** Until the batter melts in.
**Garnish.** A cinnamon stick.
