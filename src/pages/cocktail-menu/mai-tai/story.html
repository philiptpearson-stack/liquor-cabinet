Mai Tai Recipe

The Cocktail Menu · Tiki & Tropical

Mai Tai

Makes 1 drink

  • 2 oz aged rum (Jamaican, if you can find it)
  • 1 oz lime juice (fresh squeezed)
  • ½ oz orange liqueur (orange curaçao)
  • ½ oz orgeat (almond syrup)
  • ¼ oz simple syrup

Glass: rocks, over crushed ice · Garnish: mint sprig and a lime wedge

“Maita’i roa aé!”

That’s Tahitian for “out of this world, the best.”

That's what Trader Vic's friend Carrie Guild, visiting from Tahiti, said after her first sip in 1944, at his bar in Oakland, California. So that's what Vic named it.

Don the Beachcomber, Hollywood's other tiki king, claimed he'd invented it first. Vic's answer: anybody who said so was a “dirty stinker.”

It's simpler than it looks: aged rum and lime, sweetened with orange liqueur and orgeat, an almond syrup. No pineapple, no grenadine, no paper umbrella.
