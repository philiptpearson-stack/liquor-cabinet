Mai Tai Recipe

The Cocktail Menu · Tiki & Tropical

Mai Tai

Makes 1 drink

  • 2 oz aged rum (Jamaican, if you can find it)
  • 1 oz lime juice
  • ½ oz orange liqueur (orange curaçao)
  • ½ oz orgeat (almond syrup)
  • ¼ oz simple syrup

Glass: rocks, over crushed ice
Garnish: mint sprig and a lime wedge

Turns out, it's not that big pint of pineapple juice and grenadine people used to chug in the '90s.

First, it was Trader Vic's signature drink!

(In the 1940s.)

And the original formulation is sublime.

It's aged rum and lime, sweetened with orange liqueur and orgeat, an almond syrup.

Which possibly qualifies it as the most ordered and least accurately made drink of all time.
