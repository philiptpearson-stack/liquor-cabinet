Jungle Bird Recipe

The Cocktail Menu · Tiki & Tropical

Jungle Bird

Makes 1 drink

  • 1 ½ oz dark rum (blackstrap, if you can find it)
  • ¾ oz Campari
  • 1 ½ oz pineapple juice
  • ½ oz lime juice
  • ½ oz demerara syrup

Glass: rocks, over ice
Garnish: a pineapple wedge

Demerara syrup: the recipe's on our syrups page.

Campari takes a tropical vacation!
