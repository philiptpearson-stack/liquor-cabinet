Jungle Bird Recipe

The Cocktail Menu · Tiki & Tropical

Jungle Bird

Makes 1 drink

  • 1 ½ oz dark rum (blackstrap, if you can find it)
  • ¾ oz Campari
  • 1 ½ oz pineapple juice
  • ½ oz lime juice
  • ½ oz demerara syrup

Glass: rocks, over ice · Garnish: a pineapple wedge

A tiki drink with a bitter streak.

It was born around 1978 at the Kuala Lumpur Hilton, in Malaysia: dark rum, Campari, pineapple juice, lime and sugar.

Then it all but vanished, until tiki historian Jeff “Beachbum” Berry spotted it in an old paperback bartender's guide from a thrift store, and put it in his own book in 2002.

Around 2010, Giuseppe González (of Trinidad Sour fame) cut way back on the pineapple and swapped in rich, dark blackstrap rum. That's the version most bartenders make now.

The Campari is the secret. Its bitterness keeps all that rum and pineapple from getting too sweet. Balance!
