It started out in India.
And it was served cold.
In the 1600s, the British in India drank *taddy*, made from fermented palm sap.
By 1788, a “toddy” back in England meant rum, water, sugar and nutmeg. Still cold.
Somewhere along the way, somebody added hot water and whiskey, and the cold-night classic was born.
Will it cure a cold? No. But whiskey, honey, lemon and steam sure don't hurt.

**Whiskey, honey and lemon.** Add them to a warm mug.
**Top with hot water.** Just off the boil, not boiling.
**Stir.** Stir until the honey melts in.
**Garnish.** A lemon wheel.
