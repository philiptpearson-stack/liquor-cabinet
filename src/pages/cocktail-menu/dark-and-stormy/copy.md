The Cocktail Menu · Tiki & Tropical
Dark and Stormy
Makes − 1 + drink
1 ½ oz Goslings Black Seal rum (or another dark rum, but don't tell Goslings)
¾ oz lime juice
¾ oz simple syrup
4 oz ginger beer
**Glass:** highball, over ice
**Garnish:** lime wedge
**Fill with ice.** Fill a highball glass with ice.
**Lime, simple and ginger beer.** Pour in the lime juice and simple syrup, then top with the ginger beer.
**Float the rum.** Pour the Goslings slowly over the top, so it floats like a storm cloud.
**Garnish.** A lime wedge on the rim.
Pour the rum over the back of a spoon, and it'll sit right on top.
