“Tequila Has Appeala with Squirt.”
Yep.
That's an actual Squirt ad that ran in the L.A. Times in 1978.
And while most bars today will make you a fancy version with grapefruit juice, simple syrup and club soda — if you've got a Squirt, it's a more fun drink.
Here's how you make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Modern Variations
Paloma
Makes − 1 + drink
2 oz tequila
¾ oz lime juice
¾ oz simple syrup
1 pinch salt
4 oz grapefruit soda
**Glass:** highball, over ice
**Garnish:** grapefruit wedge
**Combine ingredients.** Add the tequila, lime juice, simple syrup and a pinch of salt to your shaker.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake hard, until frosty cold.
**Top with grapefruit soda.** Pour it right into the shaker, and give it a gentle swirl.
**Pour it all in.** Pour everything, ice and all, into a highball glass. The drink is already chilling on that ice.
**Garnish.** A grapefruit wedge on the rim.
