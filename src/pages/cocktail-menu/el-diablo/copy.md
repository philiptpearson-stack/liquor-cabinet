The Cocktail Menu: El ***Diablo.***
A little devil.
Trader Vic printed this one way back in 1946: tequila, lime and ginger, with a splash of blackcurrant liqueur.
Pour the crème de cassis in last. It sinks to the bottom in a deep purple swirl.
Here's how you make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Classics
El Diablo
Makes − 1 + drink
2 oz tequila
½ oz lime juice
4 oz ginger beer
½ oz crème de cassis
**Glass:** highball, over ice · **Garnish:** lime wedge
**Fill with ice.** Fill a highball glass with ice.
**Tequila and lime.** Add the tequila and lime juice.
**Top with ginger beer.**
**Drizzle in the cassis.** Pour it in slowly, and watch it sink.
**Garnish.** A lime wedge on the rim.
