It involves the Irish coast and some American tourists and a guy named Sheridan and his little coffee experiment.
But the Irish Coffee became an American institution at the iconic Buena Vista Cafe, in San Francisco, where bartenders spend all day every day working an Irish-coffee production line with an audience watching.

The Cocktail Menu · Warm & Cozy
Irish Coffee
Makes − 1 + drink
3 oz hot coffee
2 white sugar cubes
1 ¼ oz Irish whiskey
1 oz heavy cream, lightly whipped
**Glass:** a warmed Buena Vista glass (a 6 oz stemmed goblet)
**Garnish:** none (the cream is the show)
**Coffee.** Warm the glass with hot water first, and pour it out. Then fill it about two-thirds with hot coffee.
**Sugar.** Drop in two white sugar cubes, and stir until they dissolve.
**Whiskey.** Add the Irish whiskey.
**Float the cream.** Whip the cream just until it thickens but still pours. Then pour it gently over the back of a spoon, so it floats on top.
