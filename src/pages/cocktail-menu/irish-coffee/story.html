Irish Coffee

Makes 1 drink

  • 1 ½ oz Irish whiskey
  • ½ oz demerara syrup (or 2 tsp brown sugar)
  • 4 oz hot coffee
  • 1 oz heavy cream, lightly whipped

Glass: warm mug or Irish coffee glass · Garnish: none (the cream is the show)

Hot coffee, whiskey and a little sugar.

Under a cap of cold cream.

One cold, wet night in the 1940s, a flying boat full of shivering American passengers landed at Foynes, on the west coast of Ireland. Chef Joe Sheridan warmed them up with hot coffee, Irish whiskey and a cap of cream.

Somebody asked if it was Brazilian coffee.

“No,” said Sheridan. “It's Irish coffee.”

A few years later, a San Francisco newspaperman brought the idea home to the Buena Vista Café. They couldn't get the cream to float. The owner went all the way to Ireland to see how it was done.

The secret: cream whipped just until it thickens, poured gently over the back of a spoon. Don't stir it. You drink the hot coffee through the cold cream.
