A Tom Collins,
*minus the gin.*
It's the same formula as every sour on the menu, with the strong part left out. The sour and the sweet still balance each other, so it still tastes like a real drink.
(Perfect for when you want a yummy cocktail but you've got an important client presentation at 8AM the next day.)
(Which is not something we bartenders face very often, but we believe you when you tell us it happens.)
Here's how you make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Low & No-Proof
Non-Collins
Makes − 1 + drink
1 oz lemon juice
1 oz simple syrup
3 oz soda water (or your favorite fizzy water)
**Glass:** collins glass, over ice
**Garnish:** a lemon wheel
**Combine ingredients.** Add the lemon juice and simple syrup to your shaker.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake hard, until frosty cold.
**Top with soda.** Soda water, or your favorite fizzy water, right into the shaker. Give it a gentle swirl.
**Pour it all in.** Pour everything, ice and all, into a collins glass.
**Garnish.** A lemon wheel.
