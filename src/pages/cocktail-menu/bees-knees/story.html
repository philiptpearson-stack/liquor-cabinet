Bee's Knees

Makes 1 drink

  • 2 oz gin
  • 1 oz honey syrup
  • 1 oz lemon juice (fresh squeezed)

Glass: coupe · Garnish: lemon twist

Honey syrup is honey and hot water, half and half, stirred until smooth.

In the 1920s, “the bee's knees” meant the very best.

That’s a lot to live up to.

It's a Prohibition-era drink: gin, lemon and honey. Back then, the honey and lemon did a lot of work covering up the taste of bathtub gin.

Frank Meier, head bartender at the Ritz in Paris, printed it in 1936.

With good gin, it's simply the Golden Ratio with honey: the Gold Rush's older, gin-drinking cousin.
