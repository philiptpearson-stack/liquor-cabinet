Lost for generations.
Found in Seattle.
Okay you guys — this one's a real jewel.
An absolute banger from back before Prohibition, the Last Word relies on Chartreuse. And when Chartreuse (and every other spirit) disappeared from the American market, so did the Last Word.
But here's the thing: Chartreuse is a strange spirit. Made by actual Carthusian monks, it's 55% alcohol, uses 130 herbaceous ingredients, and tastes distinctly euphoric.
And nobody ever knows what to do with it.
Because it's just...weird!
Sweet.
*Very* strong.
Sort of like talking to God?
That's why (we speculate), after Seattle bartender Murray Stenson found the Last Word in an old cocktail book in 2003 and put it on the menu at the Zig Zag Café, it was so quick to take off.
The nation got a taste of the sublime.
And the Last Word got the last word after all.
Here's how you make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Arcane Oddities
Last Word
Makes − 1 + drink
¾ oz gin
¾ oz green Chartreuse
¾ oz maraschino liqueur
¾ oz lime juice
**Glass:** coupe
**Garnish:** a cherry
**Combine ingredients.** Equal parts gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur and lime juice, into your shaker.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake hard, until frosty cold.
**Strain.** Strain into a chilled coupe.
**Garnish.** A cherry.
