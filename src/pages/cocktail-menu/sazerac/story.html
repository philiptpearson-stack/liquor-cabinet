Sazerac Recipe

Sazerac

Makes 1 drink

  • 2 oz rye whiskey
  • ¼ oz–½ oz simple syrup, to taste
  • 3 dashes Peychaud's bitters
  • ¼ oz absinthe, to rinse the glass

Glass: chilled rocks glass, no ice
Garnish: lemon peel

The official cocktail of New Orleans.

By a vote of 62 to 33.

That was the Louisiana House of Representatives, in 2008. (It wasn't unanimous. We cannot understand how, or why.)

Its roots go back to the 1830s, when a French Quarter apothecary named Antoine Peychaud began mixing his family's bitters into cocktails for his friends.

Today it's a delightful “neat” Old Fashioned, just with funkier bitters and an absinthe rinse.

That absinthe rinse is the trick: pour just a splash, swirl it to coat the glass, and dump. You smell it more than taste it.
