The Cocktail Menu: ***Boulevardier.***
**Ever heard of a cocktail called Negroni?**
Italian.
Bitter.
*Strong.*
(Supposedly invented as a "strong" version of an Americano (Campari+Sweet Vermouth+Soda)).
Well, the Boulevardier is Negroni's beefy American cousin. Same delightfully bitter formula. Just made with bourbon or rye, instead of gin.
*I like a cocktail a bit on the boozier side, so I make mine with the Golden Ratio. The original recipe calls for 1:1:1. Try them both, if you like!*
→ The Golden Ratio
Here's how you make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Classics
Boulevardier
Makes − 1 + drink
2 oz bourbon or rye
1 oz sweet vermouth
1 oz Campari
**Glass:** rocks, over one big ice cube · **Garnish:** orange peel
**Combine ingredients.** Add whiskey, sweet vermouth and Campari to a mixing glass.
**Stir.** Fill with ice and stir until the drink is good and cold. Then, stir longer! (Seriously. You need some of that ice to melt. Water softens the alcohol-bite and brings the drink into **balance.** *Dilution is part of the recipe.*)
**Strain over a big cube.** Strain into a rocks glass over one large ice cube.
**Garnish.** Twist an orange peel over the drink, then drop it in.
