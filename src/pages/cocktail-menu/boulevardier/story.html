The Cocktail Menu: Boulevardier.

A Negroni in a tweed jacket.

In 1920s Paris, an American writer named Erskine Gwynne ran a magazine called Boulevardier. This was his drink.

Swap the Negroni's gin for whiskey, and everything gets warmer, rounder and a little more autumnal.

It's The Golden Ratio, with bitter in place of sour: two parts whiskey, one part sweet vermouth, one part Campari.

The Cocktail Menu · Classics

Boulevardier

Makes 1 drink

  • 2 oz bourbon or rye
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth
  • 1 oz Campari

Glass: rocks, over one big ice cube · Garnish: orange peel
