Believe it or not, the Cosmo actually had a life before** Sex and the City made it a household name.
It was invented in 1988 at the Odeon in Tribeca — the same bar that hosted Lorne Michaels' SNL after parties (*!!!*).
Why?
Bartender Toby Cecchini said he wanted *to impress the waitresses.*
We salute you, Toby.
Here's how you make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Modern Variations
Cosmopolitan
Makes − 1 + drink
1 ½ oz citrus vodka
¾ oz orange liqueur
¾ oz lime juice
½ oz cranberry juice
**Glass:** martini glass
**Garnish:** lime wedge
**Combine ingredients.** Add the citrus vodka, orange liqueur, lime juice and cranberry juice to your shaker.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake hard, until frosty cold.
**Strain.** Strain into a chilled martini glass.
**Garnish.** A lime wedge on the rim.
