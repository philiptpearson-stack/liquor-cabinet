The Cocktail Menu · Classics
Margarita
Makes − 1 + drink
2 oz tequila (blanco)
1 oz orange liqueur
1 oz lime juice
**Glass:** rocks, over ice, with a salted rim · **Garnish:** lime wheel
Salt the rim with coarse salt. Salt just half of it, if you like, so every sip can be your choice.
**Salt the rim.** Rub a lime wedge around the outside of the rim of a rocks glass, then roll it in coarse salt.
**Combine ingredients.** Add the tequila, orange liqueur and lime juice to your shaker.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake hard, until frosty cold.
**Pour it all in.** Pour everything, ice and all, into the salted glass.
**Garnish.** A lime wheel on the rim.
