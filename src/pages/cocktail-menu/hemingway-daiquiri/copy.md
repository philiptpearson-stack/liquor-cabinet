Take out the sugar and double the rum.
That's what Ernest Hemingway asked for at Havana's El Floridita, back in the 1930s.
He wanted a regular old Daiquiri, but, you know, he wanted to get...*fucked up.*
A contemporary account describes his drink as two and a half jiggers of rum, two limes, half a grapefruit, and maraschino.
Hemingway later bragged about drinking 17 (*!!!!*) of them in one afternoon.**
(Bad idea.)**
(Seriously.)**
(Please don't.)**
Today's version is...well...tastier than ol' Ernie's. And less likely to kill you from alcohol poisoning.
Here's how you make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Tiki & Tropical
Hemingway Daiquiri
Makes − 1 + drink
2 oz white rum
¾ oz lime juice
½ oz grapefruit juice
¼ oz maraschino liqueur
**Glass:** coupe
**Garnish:** a lime wheel
**Combine ingredients.** Add the rum, lime juice, grapefruit juice and maraschino to your shaker.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake hard, until frosty cold.
**Strain.** Strain into a chilled coupe.
**Garnish.** A lime wheel.
Too tart? Add ¼ oz simple syrup. (Papa would never.)
