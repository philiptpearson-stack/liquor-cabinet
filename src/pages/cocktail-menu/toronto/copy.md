It's *one* way to approach Fernet.
You know Fernet, right?
Fernet-Branca?
The massively bitter Italian bitters that bartenders order to signal to other bartenders that they, too, are bartenders.
(It's called a “bartender's handshake”) (*!!*)
Well, it's...blech-y.
But in the Toronto, it's just a little bitter in an otherwise nice Old Fashioned.
(Still blech-y. But less so. And we love Fernet!)
Here's how you make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Arcane Oddities
Toronto
Makes − 1 + drink
2 oz rye whiskey
¼ oz Fernet-Branca
¼ oz simple syrup
2 dashes Angostura bitters
**Glass:** coupe
**Garnish:** an orange twist
**Combine ingredients.** Add the rye, Fernet-Branca, simple syrup and bitters to a mixing glass.
**Stir.** Fill with ice and stir until the drink is good and cold. Then, stir longer! (Seriously. You need some of that ice to melt. Water softens the alcohol-bite and brings the drink into **balance.** *Dilution is part of the recipe.*)
**Strain.** Strain into a chilled coupe.
**Garnish.** An orange twist.
