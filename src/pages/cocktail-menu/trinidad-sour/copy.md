**Glass:** coupe · **Garnish:** none
An ounce and a half of bitters.
*On purpose.*
Most recipes call for Angostura bitters by the dash. This one uses it as the base spirit. (Angostura is about as strong as whiskey, after all.)
Bartender Giuseppe González came up with it in 2009, at the Clover Club in Brooklyn. As he put it, “nobody can imagine what it tastes like.” So everybody wants to try one.
What it tastes like: rich, spicy and surprisingly smooth. The orgeat rounds off the bitters, the lemon keeps it bright, and a little rye backs it all up.
Here's how you make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Arcane Oddities
Trinidad Sour
Makes − 1 + drink
1 ½ oz Angostura bitters
1 oz orgeat (almond syrup)
¾ oz lemon juice (fresh squeezed)
½ oz rye
**Glass:** coupe · **Garnish:** none
**Combine ingredients.** Yes, really: pour the Angostura by the jigger. Then the orgeat, lemon juice and rye.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake hard, until frosty cold.
**Strain.** Strain into a chilled coupe. No garnish needed.
