The Cocktail Menu: Old ***Fashioned.***
The original cocktail.
In 1806, a newspaper in upstate New York defined the word *cocktail*: spirits, sugar, water and bitters.
Order one the old-fashioned way, and that's exactly what you get.
Here's how to make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Classics
Old Fashioned
Makes − 1 + drink
2 oz bourbon or rye
¼ oz simple syrup
4 dashes Angostura bitters
**Glass:** rocks, over one big ice cube · **Garnish:** orange peel
**Syrup and bitters.** Add the simple syrup and bitters to a rocks glass.
**Add the whiskey.**
**Ice and stir.** Add one big ice cube and stir for about 20 seconds.
**Garnish.** Twist an orange peel over the drink, then drop it in.
Don't be shy with the bitters: start with four good dashes, stir, taste, and add more if you like.
