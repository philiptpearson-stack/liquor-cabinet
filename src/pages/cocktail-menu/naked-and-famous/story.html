Naked and Famous

Makes 1 drink

  • ¾ oz mezcal
  • ¾ oz yellow Chartreuse
  • ¾ oz Aperol
  • ¾ oz lime juice

Glass: coupe
Garnish: none

Smoky, bitter, sweet and sour.

All at once.

Joaquín Simó came up with it at Death & Co in New York in 2011, and it took off fast.

It's built just like the Last Word: four ingredients, equal parts. Only here it's smoky mezcal, honeyed yellow Chartreuse, bittersweet Aperol and lime.

Every one of them is loud. Somehow they get along.
