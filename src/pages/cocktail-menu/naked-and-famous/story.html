Naked and Famous Recipe

The Cocktail Menu · Arcane Oddities

Naked and Famous

Makes 1 drink

  • ¾ oz mezcal
  • ¾ oz yellow Chartreuse
  • ¾ oz Aperol
  • ¾ oz lime juice

Glass: coupe
Garnish: none

“It's my sluttiest recipe ever.”

That's bartender Joaquín Simó's assessment of how widely his Naked and Famous has traveled, since he invented it in 2011 at New York's Death & Co.

The drink is equal parts mezcal, Aperol, yellow Chartreuse, and lime.

Simó describes it as “a bastard love child of the Paper Plane and the Last Word.”

He's nothing if not a good quote.
