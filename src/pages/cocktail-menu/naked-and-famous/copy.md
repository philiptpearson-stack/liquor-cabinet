Smoky, bitter, sweet and sour.
*All at once.*
Joaquín Simó came up with it at Death & Co in New York in 2011, and it took off fast.
It's built just like the Last Word: four ingredients, equal parts. Only here it's smoky mezcal, honeyed yellow Chartreuse, bittersweet Aperol and lime.
Every one of them is loud. Somehow they get along.
Here's how you make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Arcane Oddities
Naked and Famous
Makes − 1 + drink
¾ oz mezcal
¾ oz yellow Chartreuse
¾ oz Aperol
¾ oz lime juice
**Glass:** coupe
**Garnish:** none
**Combine ingredients.** Equal parts mezcal, yellow Chartreuse, Aperol and lime juice, into your shaker.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake hard, until frosty cold.
**Strain.** Strain into a chilled coupe. No garnish needed.
