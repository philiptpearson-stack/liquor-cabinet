The Cocktail Menu: French ***75.***
Named after a cannon. Seriously.
The French 75 takes its name from a French field gun of World War I, because it hits just as hard.
Underneath all those bubbles, it's a little gin sour.
*It's The Golden Ratio, just smaller: one ounce of gin, a half ounce each of simple syrup and lemon. Then Champagne fills the glass.*
→ The Golden Ratio
Here's how to make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Classics
French 75
Makes − 1 + drink
1 oz gin
½ oz simple syrup
½ oz lemon juice (fresh squeezed)
3 oz Champagne, or another dry sparkling wine
**Glass:** Champagne flute · **Garnish:** lemon twist
**Combine ingredients.** Add the gin, simple syrup and lemon juice to your shaker.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake until cold.
**Strain into a flute.**
**Top with Champagne.** Pour slowly, so it doesn't foam over.
**Garnish.** A lemon twist.
