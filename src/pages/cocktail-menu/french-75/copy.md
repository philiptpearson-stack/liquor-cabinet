The Cocktail Menu: French ***75.***
The French 75 is a little gin sour. With bubbles on top!
Seriously.
*It's The Golden Ratio, just smaller!*
Visit our Golden Ratio page to revisit achieving *balance* in a cocktail →
Here's how you make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Classics
French 75
Makes − 1 + drink
1 oz gin
½ oz simple syrup
½ oz lemon juice (fresh squeezed)
3 oz Champagne, or another dry sparkling wine
**Glass:** Champagne flute · **Garnish:** lemon twist
**Combine ingredients.** Add the gin, simple syrup and lemon juice to your shaker.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake until cold.
**Strain into a flute.**
**Top with Champagne.** Pour slowly, so it doesn't foam over.
**Garnish.** A lemon twist.
