A Manhattan,
but *DARK.*
It's a great example of swapping out a single ingredient in a drink and changing the outcome dramatically.
In 2005, a San Francisco bartender swapped his Manhattan's sweet vermouth for Averna — a bittersweet amaro from Sicily — and the result was *magic*.
Black magic.
Here's how you make it:
The Cocktail Menu · Arcane Oddities
Black Manhattan
Makes − 1 + drink
2 oz rye whiskey
1 oz Averna
1 dash Angostura bitters
1 dash orange bitters
**Glass:** coupe
**Garnish:** a cherry
**Combine ingredients.** Add the rye, Averna and both bitters to a mixing glass.
**Stir.** Fill with ice and stir until the drink is good and cold. Then, stir longer! (Seriously. You need some of that ice to melt. Water softens the alcohol-bite and brings the drink into **balance.** *Dilution is part of the recipe.*)
**Strain.** Strain into a chilled coupe.
**Garnish.** A cherry.
