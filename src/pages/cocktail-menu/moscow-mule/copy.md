Introducing...vodka!
Before 1941, vodka was a hard sell in America.
Then, Smirnoff promoter John Martin met Jack Morgan, owner of Hollywood's Cock ’n’ Bull bar, and the rest was history.
The Cock ’n’ Bull was a British-style pub on L.A.’s Sunset Strip. Jack Morgan owned it, and also sold a ginger beer under the same name.
Put it in those fun copper mugs — and by 1947 the Moscow Mule was a fad drink from New York to LA.
Here's how you make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Classics
Moscow Mule
Makes − 1 + drink
1 ½ oz vodka
¾ oz lime juice
¾ oz simple syrup
4 oz ginger beer
**Glass:** copper mug, over ice · **Garnish:** lime wedge
**Combine ingredients.** Add the vodka, lime juice and simple syrup to your shaker.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake hard, until frosty cold.
**Top with ginger beer.** Pour it right into the shaker, and give it a gentle swirl.
**Pour it all in.** Pour everything, ice and all, into a copper mug. The drink is already chilling on that ice.
**Garnish.** A lime wedge on the rim.
