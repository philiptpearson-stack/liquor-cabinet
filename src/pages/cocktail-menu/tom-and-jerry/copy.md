Just the recipe, please
The Cocktail Menu · Warm & Cozy
Tom and Jerry
Makes − 1 + drink
1 oz Tom and Jerry batter (about 2 tablespoons; see below)
1 oz brandy
1 oz aged rum
4 oz hot milk
**Glass:** warm mug · **Garnish:** grated nutmeg
Batter (for about 8 drinks): separate 4 eggs. Beat the whites to stiff peaks. Beat the yolks with ½ cup sugar, ½ tsp cinnamon, ¼ tsp each ground cloves and allspice, and a splash of rum. Gently fold the two together. Make it the day you serve it. (Pasteurized eggs work fine.)
Hot eggnog, basically.
But fluffier.
It's named for Tom and Jerry, a pair of rowdy young men about town in Pierce Egan's 1821 London bestseller, Life in London. (Long before the cat and the mouse.)
Bartender Jerry Thomas printed a recipe in 1862. By the middle of the 1900s, it was a Christmas tradition, ladled from a big bowl into matching mugs with “Tom & Jerry” painted on the side. In the Midwest, it still is.
The secret is the batter: egg whites beaten stiff, folded into yolks beaten with sugar and spice. Add spirits and hot milk, and it turns into a warm, frothy cloud.
Here's how you make one:

Batter (for about 8 drinks): separate 4 eggs. Beat the whites to stiff peaks. Beat the yolks with ½ cup sugar, ½ tsp cinnamon, ¼ tsp each ground cloves and allspice, and a splash of rum. Gently fold the two together. Make it the day you serve it. (Pasteurized eggs work fine.)
**Batter in the mug.** Add a spoonful of batter to a warm mug.
**Brandy and rum.** Stir them into the batter.
**Top with hot milk.** Stir as you pour, so it foams up.
**Garnish.** Grate a little nutmeg on top.
