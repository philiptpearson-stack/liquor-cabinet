The classic hangover cure:
*Get drunk again!*
“Corpse revivers” were a whole family of morning-after drinks, going back to the 1800s.
Harry Craddock printed this one in his iconic The Savoy Cocktail Book in 1930, along with a warning:
“Four of these taken in swift succession will unrevive the corpse again.”
— Harry Craddock, The Savoy Cocktail Book, 1930
Now, we do NOT recommend drinking four in a row.
Seriously.
But one of these bad boys in a glass rinsed with absinthe? That is bright, tart, and *very* easy to drink.
Here's how you make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Arcane Oddities
Corpse Reviver No. 2
Makes − 1 + drink
¾ oz gin
¾ oz orange liqueur
¾ oz Lillet Blanc
¾ oz lemon juice
¼ oz absinthe, to rinse the glass
**Glass:** coupe, rinsed with absinthe
**Garnish:** an orange twist
**Rinse the glass.** Pour a splash of absinthe into a chilled coupe, swirl it to coat, and pour out the rest.
**Combine ingredients.** Equal parts gin, orange liqueur, Lillet Blanc and lemon juice, into your shaker.
**Shake.** Add ice and shake hard, until frosty cold.
**Strain.** Strain into the rinsed coupe.
**Garnish.** An orange twist.
