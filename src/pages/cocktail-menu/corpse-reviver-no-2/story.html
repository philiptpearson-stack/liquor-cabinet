Corpse Reviver No. 2

Makes 1 drink

  • ¾ oz gin
  • ¾ oz orange liqueur
  • ¾ oz Lillet Blanc
  • ¾ oz lemon juice (fresh squeezed)
  • ¼ oz absinthe, to rinse the glass

Glass: coupe, rinsed with absinthe · Garnish: an orange twist

A hangover cure.

Supposedly.

“Corpse revivers” were a whole family of morning-after drinks. This is the one that lived, printed by Harry Craddock in The Savoy Cocktail Book in 1930, along with a warning:

Equal parts gin, orange liqueur, Lillet Blanc and lemon, in a glass rinsed with absinthe. Bright, tart and very easy to drink.

Which is exactly why Craddock warned you.
