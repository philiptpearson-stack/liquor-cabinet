Coffee, booze and fire.
*Portland style.*
Huber's, Portland's oldest restaurant, has been setting these on fire tableside since 1975.
Owner Jim Louie borrowed the drink from a bar down the road and turned it into a show: overproof rum and triple sec, set ablaze in a sugar-rimmed glass, then coffee liqueur and hot coffee poured in from way up high.
The flame caramelizes the sugar on the rim. Whipped cream and nutmeg go on top. And the whole bar watches.
(Please don't try the three-foot pour at home.)
Here's how you make one:

Spanish Coffee
Makes − 1 + drink
¾ oz overproof rum
¼ oz orange liqueur (triple sec)
1 ½ oz coffee liqueur

**Glass:** Irish coffee glass, sugar-rimmed
**Garnish:** grated nutmeg on the cream
Careful with the fire: light it on a flat, heatproof surface, away from anything that burns. And never pour more rum into a lit glass.
**Sugar the rim.** Rub a lime wedge around the rim of an Irish coffee glass, then dip it in sugar.
**Rum and triple sec, then fire.** Add the overproof rum and triple sec, and carefully light it. Turn the glass slowly, so the flame caramelizes the sugar on the rim.
**Coffee liqueur and coffee.** Pour in the coffee liqueur (it puts out the flame), then the hot coffee.
**Cream and nutmeg.** Float the lightly whipped cream over the back of a spoon, and grate a little nutmeg on top.
