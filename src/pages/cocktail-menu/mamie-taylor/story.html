The original Moscow Mule!

Really.

Named for a popular opera singer of the 1890s, the Mamie Taylor was a full-blown national craze.

By 1900, it was the drink of the summer. Politicians, celebrities and socialites couldn't get enough.

But like so many drink fads (the Mojito of the early 2000s, the Fireball shot of the mid-2010s), it faded just as fast.

In 1904, the New York Times was already calling it “the famous Mamie Taylor of a few years ago.” It lasted until Prohibition and never came back. The Moscow Mule, from 1941, is basically its descendant.
