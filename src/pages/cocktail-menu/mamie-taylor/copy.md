The Cocktail Menu: Mamie ***Taylor.***
The original Scotch and ginger.
Named for a popular opera singer of the 1890s, the Mamie Taylor was a full-blown craze by 1900.
Decades before the Moscow Mule, this was the ginger highball everybody ordered.
Here's how you make one:
The Cocktail Menu · Classics
Mamie Taylor
Makes − 1 + drink
2 oz blended Scotch
½ oz lime juice
4 oz ginger beer
**Glass:** highball, over ice · **Garnish:** lime wheel
**Fill with ice.** Fill a highball glass with ice.
**Scotch and lime.** Add the Scotch and lime juice.
**Top with ginger beer.**
**Garnish.** A lime wheel.
