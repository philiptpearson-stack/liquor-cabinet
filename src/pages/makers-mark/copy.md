Wheated bourbon · 90 proof · Loretto, KY
Style
Wheated Kentucky straight bourbon: soft red winter wheat instead of rye, 90 proof
Founded by
Bill and Margie Samuels, 1953. First bottle in 1958.
Ownership
Family owned from 1953 to 1981. Then Hiram Walker & Sons (1981), Allied-Lyons (1987; later Allied Domecq), Fortune Brands (2005), Beam (2011), Beam Suntory (2014), renamed Suntory Global Spirits (2024).
