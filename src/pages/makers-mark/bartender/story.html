Dealer's choice

Old Fashioned

Makes 1 drink

  • 2 oz Maker's Mark
  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters
  • 1 orange peel

In a rocks glass, over one big ice cube

  1. Add the simple syrup and bitters to a rocks glass.
  2. Pour in the Maker's.
  3. Add one big ice cube and stir for about 20 seconds, until the glass feels cold.
  4. Squeeze the orange peel over the drink, skin side down, so its oils mist across the top. Rub it around the rim and drop it in.
