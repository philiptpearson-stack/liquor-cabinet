The Bartender's ***Perspective***
Ooo Maker's Mark?
Hell yes.
Wheated bourbons can be excellent in cocktails!
They're soft, sweet, and they play nicely with other ingredients.
Now, if what you want is a very assertive bourbon profile for your cocktail, Maker's is probably not the right choice.
But for the cocktail I'd like to share with you today, Maker's is perfect.
Now, for my favorite part. *tasting the ingredients!*
From the Cocktail Menu
Classic Whiskey Sour
Makes − 1 + drink
2 oz Maker's Mark
1 oz lemon juice (fresh squeezed or pasteurized)
1 oz simple syrup
1 egg white (optional)
**Combine ingredients.** Add everything to your shaker.
**Dry shake.** Shake hard, without ice, for about 15 seconds. That's what whips the egg white into foam.
**Wet shake.** Add ice and shake again, until the shaker is frosty cold.
**Serve up** (with a nice egg white foam). Strain into a chilled coupe, no ice.
