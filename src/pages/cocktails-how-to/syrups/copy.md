Cocktail Basics and How-To's: Homemade Syrups.
Almost every drink on our menu needs something sweet.
Usually, that's a syrup.
**Here's how you can make them at home:**
Cocktail Basics · Syrups
Simple Syrup
1 cup sugar
1 cup water
How: Boil the water. Add the sugar and stir until it's completely dissolved. Let it cool.
Keeps: about a month in the fridge
In our
Daiquiri
,
Tom Collins
,
Whiskey Sour
and 17 more drinks.
Just boil a little water, add your sugar, and stir.
Cocktail Basics · Syrups
Demerara Syrup
1 cup demerara sugar (or turbinado)
1 cup water
How: Just like simple syrup. The crystals are bigger, so it takes a little more stirring.
Keeps: about a month in the fridge
In our
Old Fashioned
and
Jungle Bird
.
It's richer, with a little caramel to it. Perfect with whiskey and dark rum.
Cocktail Basics · Syrups
Honey Syrup
1 cup honey
1 cup hot water
How: Stir until smooth.
Keeps: about a month in the fridge
In our
Bee's Knees
and
Gold Rush
.
Why not just use regular honey? Too thick! This syrup trick lets it mix easily into your cocktails.
Cocktail Basics · Syrups
Passion Fruit Syrup
1 cup passion fruit purée
1 cup sugar
How: Stir over low heat until the sugar dissolves. Let it cool.
Keeps: about two weeks in the fridge
In our
Hurricane
.
Look for frozen passion fruit purée in the freezer aisle. (Sometimes it's labeled maracuyá.)
Cocktail Basics · Syrups
Grenadine
1 cup 100% unsweetened pomegranate juice (POM works)
1 cup white sugar
¼ tsp orange-blossom water
1 tbsp pomegranate molasses
How: Warm the juice, sugar and pomegranate molasses over low heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves. Don't let it boil. Let it cool, then stir in the orange-blossom water.
Keeps: about two weeks in the fridge
In our
Jack Rose
,
Pink Lady
and
Bridge City Lemonade
.
Real grenadine is pomegranate syrup. The bright red stuff at the grocery store? It's sweet and colorful but not what these recipes are calling for.
That's the homemade stuff. Tart, deep, and delicious.
Cocktail Basics · Syrups
Honey-Ginger Syrup
1 cup honey
1 cup water
3 inches fresh ginger, peeled and thinly sliced
How: Simmer it all for 5 minutes. Turn off the heat, let it cool, then strain out the ginger.
Keeps: about two weeks in the fridge
In our
Penicillin
. (No time? For one drink, use ½ oz honey syrup and ¼ oz fresh ginger juice.)
Want a raspberry syrup? Same method.
Simmer the raspberries with sugar and water, let it cool, and strain.
Want a BLUEBERRY syrup? Same method! Simmer the blueberries with sugar and water, let it cool, and strain.
Cinnamon? Rosemary? Jalapeño? You guessed it: same method.
