Cocktail Basics and How-To's: Homemade Syrups.

The Short Version
1 : 1 · sugar : water
Stir. Cool. Keep it in the fridge.
The recipe
Equal parts sugar and hot water, stirred until the sugar disappears. That's simple syrup.
Everything else
Every other syrup here is a riff on it: a different sugar, or a little something extra.

Almost every drink on our menu needs something sweet.

Usually, that's a syrup.

And you can make every one of them at home.

(Most of them in about two minutes.)

Start here: Simple Syrup

Simple Syrup

  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup hot water

How: Stir (or shake it up in a jar) until the sugar's completely dissolved. Let it cool.
Keeps: about a month in the fridge

In our Daiquiri, Tom Collins, Whiskey Sour and 17 more drinks.

Hot water from the tap works fine. No stove required!

Swap the sugar

Use a different sweetener, and you get a whole different flavor.

Demerara Syrup

  • 1 cup demerara sugar (or turbinado)
  • 1 cup hot water

How: Just like simple syrup. The crystals are bigger, so it takes a little more stirring.
Keeps: about a month in the fridge

In our Old Fashioned and Jungle Bird.

It's richer, with a little caramel to it. Perfect with whiskey and dark rum.

Honey Syrup

  • 1 cup honey
  • 1 cup hot water

How: Stir until smooth.
Keeps: about a month in the fridge

In our Bee's Knees and Gold Rush.

Why not just use honey? Because cold honey turns into a sticky glob in your shaker.

(The Hot Toddy is the exception. The hot water takes care of it.)

Honey-Ginger Syrup

  • 1 cup honey
  • 1 cup water
  • 3 inches fresh ginger, peeled and thinly sliced

How: Simmer it all for 5 minutes. Turn off the heat, let it cool, then strain out the ginger.
Keeps: about two weeks in the fridge

In our Penicillin. (No time? For one drink, use ½ oz honey syrup and ¼ oz fresh ginger juice.)

Real grenadine is pomegranate syrup. (Grenade is French for pomegranate!)

The bright red stuff at the grocery store? Mostly corn syrup and food coloring.

Homemade is deep, tart and a whole lot better.

Cocktail Basics · Syrups

Grenadine

  • 1 cup pomegranate juice (100% juice, nothing added)
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ½ tsp orange flower water (optional)

How: Warm the juice and sugar over low heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves. Don't let it boil. Let it cool, then stir in the orange flower water.
Keeps: about two weeks in the fridge

In our Jack Rose, Pink Lady and Bridge City Lemonade.

Passion Fruit Syrup

  • 1 cup passion fruit purée
  • 1 cup sugar

How: Stir over low heat until the sugar dissolves. Let it cool.
Keeps: about two weeks in the fridge

In our Hurricane.

Look for frozen passion fruit purée in the freezer aisle. (Sometimes it's labeled maracuyá.)

And one you can just buy

Orgeat (say it “or-ZHAHT”) is an almond syrup, with a splash of orange flower water. It's the secret behind the Mai Tai.

Making it from scratch means soaking, blending and straining almonds. Which you can totally do!

But a good bottle from the liquor store is a lot easier. Look for one made with real almonds.

Or, try this shortcut:

Cocktail Basics · Syrups

Orgeat (the Shortcut)

  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ¼ tsp orange flower water
  • 1 drop almond extract

How: Warm the almond milk and sugar over low heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves. Let it cool, then stir in the orange flower water and almond extract.
Keeps: about a week in the fridge (shake it before you pour)

In our Mai Tai.
